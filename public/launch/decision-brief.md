Original decision status: draft
Owner: Demo product team

## Problem
FICTIONAL ROUND B: Four of five reviewed overdue cases lacked required documents, while one was overlooked. For these invented cases, request readiness is a stronger explanation than missed notifications. This does not establish population prevalence.

## Options and choice
Chosen: Request readiness checklist — synthetic

## Reasoning
Choose a small readiness-checklist experiment despite reminders ranking first under the original estimates. The new fictional case review suggests incomplete inputs are the first problem to investigate. Keep reminders available for complete requests that are still overlooked.

## Tradeoffs
All evidence is synthetic. A five-case convenience sample cannot prove prevalence or checklist effectiveness. Reminders may still help overlooked requests, but reminder-only work misses incomplete requests. Adoption, willingness to pay, and measured benefit remain unknown.

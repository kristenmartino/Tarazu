TARAZU PRODUCT FEEDBACK WALKTHROUGH
Prepared October 24, 2026 • https://tarazu.app/demo

Open the no-account Relay demo. Each visitor gets a separate browser copy of its evidence, saved brief and two decision drafts. Relay, its users, interviews, case reviews, baselines and forecasts are fictional. This demonstrates a decision workflow, not customer validation or measured results.

Opening sentence
“Tarazu keeps the evidence, assumptions and reasoning behind a product decision easy to revisit. This fictional team starts with a reminder idea and chooses a smaller experiment before committing to a build.”

THE ONE-MINUTE ROUTE
Choose “Start the decision walkthrough”. It opens a read-only prepared story, not an editing form. Use Next and Back or choose a named step.

1. Problem
Administrators are chasing overdue approvals. Reminders sound useful, but the cause is still an assumption.

2. Evidence
One fictional source supports overlooked requests. Other sources suggest missing information. An invented five-case review found four incomplete requests; that small convenience sample does not establish prevalence or prove a solution works.

3. Decision
Propose testing request readiness before a reminder-only build. Historical decision snapshots preserve earlier scopes and estimates; today's calibrated ranking is a different comparison. Do not say that evidence alone mathematically reranked the options.

4. Next test
Propose a paper checklist test and record what to observe next. This is planned research, not completed validation. “Open the saved decision” opens the relevant draft, with Problem, Evidence, Decision and Next test sections. The source brief revision and full original record remain available.

The walkthrough is the prepared original example. If a visitor edits their browser copy, distinguish this fixed example from their current workspace records. Reading or stepping through the story does not save, approve or change work.

Ask one question
“Think of your last difficult product decision: which part of this would have helped, and where would it have added work?”

OPTIONAL DEEPER INSPECTION
Open “Explore the original records” for these deeper actions. Use them only when a reviewer wants detail. Do not make a hiring manager complete an editing form to understand the story.

Scoring assumptions
“Inspect scoring assumptions” explicitly opens the reminder candidate form. The shared period is a 30-day pilot, the audience is 100 hypothetical users and capacity is one engineer for two weeks. Reach 55 is a band score; its selected estimate forecasts 60 of 100 users. Impact 55 maps to a forecast change from 30% to 25% overdue, a five-percentage-point difference. Effort 35 is an estimate of three total person-days, not elapsed duration. Confidence 20 is an evidence band, not a success probability. Read full claims for their caveats and linked sources. Cancel without accepting new AI values during the prepared walkthrough.

Current comparison
Priorities ranks the paper readiness-checklist exercise at 7,500, the reminder pilot at 1,729 and the read-only status dashboard pilot at 191. These are relative indices, not benefit ratios or ROI. The scopes differ: one product/design day, three total person-days and ten total person-days respectively. Production builds would require further scope and engineering review.

Evidence
“Inspect the evidence” opens Signals. Show both support and conflict. Administrator samples overlap; they are not independent votes. The small fictional case review weakens a reminder-only explanation without proving checklist effectiveness, causal benefit or population prevalence.

Saved investigation
“Open the saved brief” opens Validate; continue the latest saved brief if needed. Revision 5 contains the added Signal, changed assumption, risks and proposed next test. Four of seven dimensions have illustrative ratings; market pull, differentiation and monetization remain unassessed. Saving a brief is separate from approving a decision.

Historical decisions
Round A preserves brief revision 4 and two sources. Round B preserves revision 5 and three sources. Expanded decisions show the frozen problem, evidence, recorded choice and next test. The original full rationale and other details remain inspectable with their line breaks and lists. A revised current brief or live Signal must not replace a decision's frozen record. Both prepared decisions are drafts, not approved builds or observed outcomes.

OPTIONAL LIVE AI

These are live sanity checks, not model-accuracy benchmarks. The guest story makes no model calls and has no generated fallback.

PERSONAL USE AND HELP
“Start your own decision” opens a blank candidate in the current workspace and preserves existing work. The Relay copy stays separate. Guest work saves in this browser; sign in first for cloud saving and live AI. /contact offers email drafts and data help. /privacy explains storage, providers and optional aggregate measurement. CSV/PDF are limited exports, not a complete workspace backup.

# Tarazu launch-day FAQ

Prepared October 25, 2026. Suggested replies for Kristen to adapt to the question; these have not been posted. Check current pricing and service status before answering.

## What does Tarazu do, and who is it for?

Tarazu helps you explain a product choice and revisit it when you learn something new. Compare options, connect evidence and assumptions, save a validation brief, and record the decision and its later reviews. It is intended for solo builders and small product teams with an active decision to make. Early access is a chance to learn whether that workflow helps with real decisions.

## Where should I start?

Try the [Relay example](https://tarazu.app/demo) without an account. The short walkthrough follows a problem, conflicting evidence, a draft choice and the next test. Then choose **Start your own decision** for a blank candidate. The example stays separate from your own work. [Watch the walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AgdAIZlUxr8) if you prefer a video.

## Is the demo real customer research?

No. Relay, its people, research and case reviews are fictional. The checklist experiment is proposed and has not been run. The example demonstrates the workflow; it is not evidence of customer demand or business results. The video uses actual app still captures, rather than a live AI session.

## How is this different from a spreadsheet or an AI chat?

A spreadsheet may be enough if you only need a ranked list. Tarazu keeps the sources, assumptions, validation brief and decision reviews together so you can explain why you chose an option and what changed later. AI can help draft scores and questions, while the saved reasoning gives you something to review and share. Tarazu does not replace your delivery tracker.

## Does AI decide what to build? How are scores estimated?

You decide. AI uses the context you supply: the Strategy Brief, shared scoring anchors, candidate-specific answers, recorded claims, linked Signals and related decisions. Tarazu uses four 1–100 RICE inputs; those are relative ratings, not automatically measured customer counts or person-months. AI can suggest scores, show its basis and ask a follow-up question. Review the cited material and assumptions before accepting anything. A precise-looking score is not proof of demand or a reliable forecast.

## Can I use it without AI or an account?

Yes. Guest/manual work saves in this browser. Sign in first for cloud saving and live AI. Clearing site data can remove browser-only work, and signing in does not silently import it into your account. The fictional guest demo never calls live AI.

## What does it cost, and what if AI is unavailable?

Tarazu is [free during early access](https://tarazu.app/pricing). Future pricing and feature availability may change; planned paid tiers cannot currently be purchased. Signed-in AI has per-account, shared-capacity and spending limits. A shared limit or provider problem can make it temporarily unavailable; manual scoring and saved-record workflows remain available. I am not promising unlimited AI or a response-time guarantee.

## Where does my data go?

Guest work stays in browser storage. Clerk handles sign-in, Supabase stores signed-in workspace content, and Vercel hosts the app. Running AI sends relevant workspace context through Tarazu's server to Anthropic. Do not include information you are not permitted to share with those services. Optional product measurement is off until allowed in the browser. See [Privacy and data](https://tarazu.app/privacy) for details, retention and controls.

## Can I export or share my work?

You can copy a saved decision as a Markdown brief with its preserved evidence and dated reviews. Review it for private information before sharing. CSV contains the ranked candidate table and PDF covers the priority view; those exports omit Signals, scoring claims, saved validation briefs and decision history. There is no complete workspace backup or public shareable workspace link.

## Was AI used to build Tarazu?

Yes. I used AI coding assistance during development. My role includes the product choices, acceptance criteria and review of the resulting behavior. The [case study](https://tarazu.app/case-study) explains the choices, tradeoffs and lessons. AI assistance does not establish production reliability or customer results; I describe those only where they have been checked.

## How can I report a problem or request data help?

Use [Feedback and contact](https://tarazu.app/contact). Include the page, browser, what you expected and what happened. Do not post private research, passwords or account details in a Product Hunt comment. The contact links open a draft in your email app; they do not send automatically. For cloud-data help or deletion, write from your account email so ownership can be verified before changes.

## What feedback would be most useful?

Tell me about a product choice you are making now: what you compared, what evidence you had, and where Tarazu helped or added work. I would especially like to learn whether you return to the saved decision when new information arrives.

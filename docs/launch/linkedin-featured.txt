POST DRAFT — NOT PUBLISHED
I’ve been building Tarazu around a question: when a product decision changes, can you still explain what you believed, what evidence you had, and why you chose that next step?

The working product connects scoring assumptions to evidence, keeps reported facts separate from estimates and AI assumptions, and saves validation briefs and decision rationale for later review.

The case study explains three choices and their tradeoffs: making scoring assumptions visible, preserving the evidence behind each decision, and showing a short story before the full editor.

I’ve made a complete fictional example available without signing in. It follows a team investigating approval delays and comparing what to test before committing to a build.

I’m looking for product, design and business feedback, especially from people with a recent difficult test-or-build decision. Where would this help, and where would it add work?

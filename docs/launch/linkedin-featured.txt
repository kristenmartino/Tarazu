I built Tarazu around a question that comes after prioritization: “Why did we choose this?”

The fictional demo starts with approval reminders. Notes about missing documents suggest a different problem, so the team chooses a checklist experiment before building anything.

Tarazu keeps that choice, its sources and later reviews together. You can copy the brief into a document or bring it back when the situation changes.

The case study covers what I chose to build, what I changed after feedback, and how I used AI coding tools.

I’d like feedback from people making a product decision now: would this help you explain your choice, or add work?

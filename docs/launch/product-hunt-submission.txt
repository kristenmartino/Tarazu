Maker first comment
Hi Product Hunt — I’m Kristen, Tarazu’s maker.

I built Tarazu around the question that follows a ranked backlog: why did we choose this?

In the fictional Relay demo, a team starts with approval reminders. Notes about missing documents point to a different problem, so the team chooses a paper checklist experiment before building anything. The saved record keeps the choice and its sources together. You can copy it into a document and add a dated review later.

AI can suggest scores and questions from the context you supply. You choose what to accept.

The demo research is fictional and the experiment has not been run. I’d like feedback from people making a product decision now: would this help you explain your choice, or add work?

Guest work saves in your browser. Sign in first for cloud saving and live AI. AI can be slow, wrong or temporarily unavailable within shared limits; manual work remains available. Copy a decision brief with its preserved evidence and reviews. CSV and PDF cover the priority view/candidate table; none is a full workspace backup.
